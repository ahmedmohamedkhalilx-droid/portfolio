  1. 01

    Every email the firm sent was unauthenticated.

    The domain had no SPF record and no DMARC record at all, so mail from it, including form submissions, was landing in spam. Diagnosed against public DNS rather than the host's own panel, then added both records at the zone.

    Authenticated · verified in public DNS
  2. 02

    The contact and CV forms had nowhere to go.

    A static site with no backend. Built a PHP mail pipeline with file attachments for CVs, honeypot and timing checks instead of a CAPTCHA, header-injection stripping, and separate routing so applications reach HR and enquiries reach the business inbox.

    Tested end to end against the live endpoint
  3. 03

    The site was unusable on an iPhone in ways no emulator showed.

    iOS Safari withholds click events from elements it doesn't treat as interactive, so cards that passed every desktop test did nothing under a real thumb. Separately, every form field was under 16px, which makes Safari zoom in on focus and never zoom back out.

    24 undersized tap targets → 0
  4. 04

    Visitors kept being served an old copy of the site.

    Deploys reported success while phones still showed a build hours old. The host's cache was replaying stale pages and ignoring every standard instruction to stop, which its own responses proved. Fixed by finding the one instruction it does obey.

    Deploys now reach visitors immediately
  5. 05

    Search engines had almost nothing to work with.

    No sitemap, no robots file, no canonical tags, no structured data, and HTTP served alongside HTTPS as duplicate content. Added the full technical layer, including hreflang across both locales so the Arabic and English pages stop competing.

    18 pages indexed cleanly, two languages
  6. 06

    Text that failed to meet contrast minimums.

    The one cue telling phone users the sector cards opened was the least readable thing on the card, at 2.8:1, below the accessibility floor. Raised it, and audited every colour pair on the site rather than just that one.

    2.8:1 → 5.32:1

How I check my own work

Method

Measured, not eyeballed

Contrast ratios computed, tap targets measured in pixels, page weight counted. Every number on this page came from a measurement taken, not an impression.

Checked on the live site, not my laptop

A cache bug on the HELCO job taught me that testing with a cache-busting URL proves nothing about what a visitor receives. I verify the plain address, the one people actually type.

Written down so it can be checked

Every change is committed with the reason it was made, so you or the next developer can see why something is the way it is.

Automatic link checking

A crawler written for the project walks every page in both languages before each release. The HELCO site ships with zero dead links or dead anchors across 18 pages.
